5. ZOD SCHEMAS (frontend/src/features/venues/schemas/venue.schemas.ts)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- VenueVisibilitySchema: z.enum(["PUBLIC", "PRIVATE"])
- VenueSchema: Validate day du cac truong cua entity Venue (UUID, name, address, city, country, etc.)
- CreateVenueRequestSchema: Validate du lieu tao moi (name, address, city, country bat buoc; toa do trong pham vi hop le)
- UpdateVenueRequestSchema: Validate du lieu cap nhat (cac truong deu optional)
- VenueSummarySchema: Validate response dang tom tat (VenueSummaryResponse)

6. DIEM LECH (MISMATCH) CAN LUU Y GIUA FRONTEND & BACKEND
----------------------------------------------------------
a. Truong 'capacity':
   - Da loai bo hoan toan khoi Venue vi capacity thuoc ve map_regions, khong thuoc bang venues.
b. Truong 'createdBy':
   - Database co cot created_by (UUID), entity Venue.java co createdBy.
   - DTO VenueResponse.java cua backend hien chua tra ve createdBy.
   - Frontend xu ly: khai bao createdBy?: string | null de tranh loi khi backend khong tra ve.
c. Truong 'active' vs 'isActive':
   - DB co cot is_active, entity dung active = true map toi is_active.
   - Record VenueResponse.java su dung ten 'active'. Khi serialize ra JSON qua Jackson se thanh 'active'.
   - Frontend xu ly: khai bao isActive: boolean kem active?: boolean de ho tro ca hai dang.
